A flexible radio player for the way you actually listen.

Discover stations, bring your own compatible streams, save what you like, listen locally or cast it to another device, and build StreamBeam into your daily listening routine.

01 — DISCOVER

Find something to listen to.

Discover radio by station, country, location, genre, and more. Browse what's available and find something worth keeping.

02 — BRING YOUR OWN

Not in the directory? Bring it with you.

Add a compatible stream URL, give it a name and icon, and make it part of your StreamBeam library.

03 — SAVE

Keep the streams you actually listen to.

Save stations and custom streams so the things you listen to most are always within reach.

04 — PLAY

Just press play.

A clean Now Playing experience brings together station information, live metadata, artwork, and playback controls without getting in the way of listening.

05 — BEAM

Your audio. Your screen. Your speakers.

Listen directly on your iPhone or iPad, use AirPlay, or send compatible streams to Google Cast devices—all from one place.

Set a Radio Alarm with the station you choose, or use Sleep Timer when you’re done listening.
