StreamBeam for iPhone & iPad

Find it.
Play it.
Beam it.

Radio and streams, without the usual boundaries.

Discover stations from around the world, bring your own compatible streams, and move listening between your Apple devices, AirPlay and Google Cast.

Coming soon to the App Store
01 — DISCOVER

Radio without borders.

Search by station, genre, city or country. Browse local radio, explore by country, discover popular stations, and keep finding something new to listen to.

02 — PLAY

More than a station name.

StreamBeam brings station information, live track metadata, artwork and playback controls together in a clean Now Playing experience.

03 — YOUR STREAMS

Not in the directory? Add it yourself.

Have a compatible direct stream URL you already listen to? Add it to StreamBeam and make it part of your library—right alongside discovered stations and favorites.

04 — BEAM

Your audio. Your screen. Your speakers.

Listen directly on your iPhone or iPad, move playback to AirPlay, or beam compatible streams to Google Cast devices—all from one place.

05 — WHEN YOU LISTEN

Wake up to it. Drift off to it.

Set a radio alarm with the station you choose, including an option to try your last-used Google Cast device, or let a sleep timer stop whichever playback destination is active.
