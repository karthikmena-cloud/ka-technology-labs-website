Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 24, 2026

Pre-release draft. Review this page against the final shipping build and App Store privacy disclosures before submission.

KA Technology Labs LLC respects your privacy. This policy describes privacy practices for StreamBeam and related support services.

Information you provide

If you contact us for support, we may receive information you choose to provide, such as your email address and message.

App functionality and third-party services

StreamBeam connects to internet radio streams, directory services, artwork or metadata sources, and device playback technologies. Requests to those services may necessarily include technical information such as an IP address as part of normal internet communication. Those services may have their own privacy practices.

Local data

StreamBeam may store preferences and listening-related information on your device for features such as saved streams, favorites, recents, alarms, and settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent to contact@katechnologylabs.com.
