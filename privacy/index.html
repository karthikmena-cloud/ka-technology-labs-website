Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KA Technology Labs LLC handles information when you visit katechnologylabs.com, contact us, or use StreamBeam, our internet-radio and audio-streaming application.

Information you provide

If you contact us for support, feedback, or other inquiries, we receive the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, and any attachments you include.

Website information

Our website is a static website hosted through GitHub Pages. Like other web hosting services, the hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser information, and request timestamps, to deliver and secure the website.

StreamBeam information stored on your device

StreamBeam does not require an account and does not collect profile information or create user profiles.

StreamBeam uses device storage to support features such as saved stations, custom stream URLs, favorites, recent listening, alarms, sleep timer settings, and application preferences.

Location information

StreamBeam may request location permission to help identify nearby radio stations. Location information is processed on your device and is not transmitted to KA Technology Labs.

Third-party services and internet connections

StreamBeam may connect to radio-directory services, station websites, stream providers, and metadata or artwork providers.

Those services may receive technical information necessary to fulfill your request, such as your device's public IP address as part of normal internet communication. KA Technology Labs does not receive or store this information.

Google Cast and local network features

StreamBeam may use local network access to discover and connect to compatible playback devices, including Google Cast devices. Device discovery and connection can involve local-network information. This information is used to provide playback functionality and is not collected by KA Technology Labs.

Children's privacy

StreamBeam is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our products evolve. The effective date above identifies the current version.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to contact@katechnologylabs.com.
