Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how KA Technology Labs LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you visit katechnologylabs.com, contact us, or use StreamBeam, our internet-radio and audio-streaming application.

1. Information you provide

When you email us for support, feedback, or another inquiry, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name, email address, message, and any attachments. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information in a support message.

2. Website information

Our website is a static site hosted using GitHub Pages. Like other web-hosting services, the hosting infrastructure may process technical request information, such as IP address, browser information, and request timestamps, to deliver and secure the site. We do not currently provide account registration through this website.

3. StreamBeam information stored on your device

StreamBeam uses device storage to support features such as saved stations and custom stream URLs, favorites, recent listening, alarms, sleep timer, playback preferences, and other settings. If you choose to export a backup or save it to Files or iCloud Drive, that file is handled according to your selected storage destination and its applicable privacy settings. We do not receive your backup merely because you create or save it.

4. Internet radio and external services

To discover or play stations and streams, StreamBeam may connect to radio-directory services, station websites, stream providers, and metadata or artwork providers. Those requests can expose ordinary connection information, including your device's public IP address, to the relevant service. The availability, content, and privacy practices of third-party services are outside our control. Artwork and metadata providers used by the released app may change as we update the product.

5. Playback and connected devices

When you choose local playback, AirPlay, or Google Cast, StreamBeam communicates with the relevant device, platform, and/or streaming endpoint to provide playback. Device discovery and connection can involve local-network information. Your use of Apple and Google services is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

6. How we use information

We use information you send us to respond to requests, investigate reported issues, improve our products, and maintain the security and operation of our services. We do not sell the personal information submitted to us through support inquiries.

7. Sharing and retention

We may share information with service providers as reasonably necessary to host the website, operate communications, and respond to support requests; when required by applicable law; or to protect our rights and users. We retain support correspondence only as reasonably needed for those purposes and applicable legal obligations. Data stored locally in StreamBeam generally remains on your device unless you export or share it.

8. Your choices

You can edit or remove saved items and settings through available app controls, remove the app from your device, and manage backups through the storage service you selected. To request access to or deletion of information you previously sent us directly, contact us using the email address below. We will respond in accordance with applicable law.

9. Children

StreamBeam is a general-audience audio application and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our products, service providers, or practices change. We will post the revised policy here with an updated date.
